The bundled muscles.glb is an unmodified copy of the web adaptation by
tiralonghipol for Free Muscles, commit 605dd1ca351421ad874a03f673a2a3f915ab254e.
https://github.com/tiralonghipol/free-muscles/tree/605dd1ca351421ad874a03f673a2a3f915ab254e
The upstream adaptation extracts muscles and bones, removes connective
tissue, reduces polygons, adds group metadata and compresses the geometry.
The bundled muscles-female.glb is the illustrative female variant from
Fit Mit With, commit 4120ee68b6604b8f2f69105d6de6166fad4734c4.
https://github.com/slfresh/fitmitwith-anatomy-atlas/tree/4120ee68b6604b8f2f69105d6de6166fad4734c4
See FEMALE-ATTRIBUTION.txt for the complete upstream notices and adaptation
details. The variant is a deformation of the original male geometry with
illustrative proportions.

At runtime МиоАтлас groups surfaces, adjusts figure dimensions, replaces the
visible head with a custom mannequin shell and adds display materials,
selection highlights and camera controls.

The bundled models and their anatomical adaptations are available under CC BY-SA 4.0.
